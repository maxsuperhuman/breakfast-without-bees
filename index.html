How healthy is your breakfast?

A good breakfast gives about a quarter of what you need in a day. Goals use the reference values printed on EU food labels (vitamin C 80 mg, folate 200 µg, protein 50 g a day), 25 g of fibre a day, and at most 30 g of added sugars a day (UK advice for ages 11 and up).

Added sugars = sugars from sugar, honey, syrups, jam, juice, cookies and chocolate; sugar inside whole fruit and milk is not counted. “Bee share of nutrients”: each nutrient is measured in breakfast goals, then we see what part comes from pollinators. Nutrition values are typical values per 100 g and are approximate.
