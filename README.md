# Breakfast without bees

Build your breakfast and see how much of it depends on pollinators, by weight and by price, compared with 10 national breakfasts.

Research project by Matvei Medvedev, Limassol, Cyprus. Pollinator dependence classes: Klein et al. (2007). Prices: estimated Cyprus prices per kilo.

Live: https://maxsuperhuman.github.io/breakfast-without-bees/
